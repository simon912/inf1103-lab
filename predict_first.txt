Supplier and Product Association
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prediction: Expected each Product object to store a reference to one Supplier object and also expected the Supplier to exist
independently without being owned by Product

What Happened: Test shows that both PhysicalProduct and DigitalProduct referenced the same Supplier object, thus Supplier can 
be created independently.

OrderLine and Product Association
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prediction: Expected each OrderLine object to reference an existing Product without owning or controlling the Product's lifecycle.

What Happened: OrderLine successfully referenced the original Product. Removing OrderLine did not remove the Product from Inventory.

Changes:
- Added get_product() to OrderLine so that other classes can retrieve without accessing private attributes directly.
